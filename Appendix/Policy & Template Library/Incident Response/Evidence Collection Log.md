## Evidence Handling Principles

- Preserve originals where practical.

- Work from copies where appropriate.

- Avoid unnecessary modification.

- Restrict access.

- Record who collected important evidence.

- Record where it is stored.

- Avoid deleting logs or affected accounts before evidence needs are considered.

- Seek specialist advice where legal proceedings or formal forensic investigation may be involved.

## Practical Rule

Evidence should help answer:

What happened, when did it happen, what was affected, and what actions were taken?
