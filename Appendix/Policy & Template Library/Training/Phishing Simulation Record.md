## Management of Results

Results should be handled proportionately.

Avoid using simulation results as the sole measure of employee cybersecurity performance.

**Where possible, focus on:**

- Improving reporting
- Identifying repeated weaknesses
- Improving technical controls
- Providing additional training
- Recognizing positive reporting behavior
