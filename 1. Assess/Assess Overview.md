Potential sections

## 1. Assess

Purpose: Understand the company’s current cybersecurity posture, business risks, and readiness level.

### 1.1 Business Context Assessment

Define what the business does, what systems support operations, what data matters most, and which processes must keep running during disruption.

### 1.2 Cybersecurity Maturity Assessment

Review the current state of security controls across people, process, and technology.

Include:

MFA coverage  
Backup maturity  
Endpoint protection  
Patch management  
Email security  
Vendor security  
Incident response readiness  
Employee awareness

### 1.3 Risk Assessment

Identify the most likely and most damaging cyber scenarios for the SME.

Examples:

Business email compromise  
Ransomware  
Credential theft  
Cloud account takeover  
Lost or stolen laptop  
Invoice fraud  
Data breach  
Website compromise  
Third-party compromise

### 1.4 Compliance and Contractual Assessment

List applicable obligations from clients, regulators, insurers, and industry standards.

### 1.5 Gap Analysis

Compare current controls against the desired minimum security baseline.

Output of this phase:

Current-state assessment  
Risk register  
Critical gaps list  
Initial security priorities
